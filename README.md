# InstagramLikesDeleter

**WHAT**

<!-- -->
This tool simply removes your previous "likes" on Instagram in a systematic way. 

<!-- -->
<!-- -->
IG is selling your data to databrokers and your likes form part of that profile.

<!-- -->
It may be too late to put the toothpaste back in the tube, but its never to late to begin a good digital hygeine. Deleting all the old info on IG is a very good start.

<!-- -->
It is designed to be thorough, not fast. Leave the tool to putter away deleting those likes

<!-- -->
**USAGE**

1. Open a console on your browser
2. Agree to the terms it may present
3. Type any requested override text such as "allow pasting"
4. Copy/paste the code and hit enter.

<!-- -->
Code will delete 20 likes at a time with a 60 second delay between each iteration.
